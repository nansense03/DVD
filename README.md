## About this collection

We love movies, and we love owning them. There's something special about a real disc: the cover art, the special features, the ritual of picking something off the shelf and pressing play. Streaming services come and go, and titles disappear without warning. A physical copy stays put.

This site is our shelf, made searchable. It's a living catalogue of the physical movies and media we've gathered over time, and it keeps growing. Every time something new joins the collection, it appears here too.

Whether you're a friend looking for something to borrow, a fellow collector curious about what we've found, or just us trying to remember whether we already own a particular film, this is the place to look.

---

## Finding something

**Search.** Start typing in the search bar at the top and the shelf narrows as you go. You can search by title, by what kind of media it is, or by the names of the lead actor, lead actress or director.

**Filter.** Use the dropdowns under the search bar to narrow things down by:

- **Genre**: drama, comedy, thriller and so on. Films with more than one genre show up under each of them.
- **Language**: the language the film was made in.
- **Format**: how the title is stored, such as a particular disc type.
- **Decade**: the era the film was released in, from the classics to the latest.

You can combine search and filters. For example, search for a director and then filter by decade to see their work from one era. Active filters appear as small tags beneath the dropdowns, and clicking any tag removes it.

---

## Reading a card

Each title on the shelf is shown as a card.

- **Poster**: the film's artwork. If we don't have one, you'll see the title on a plain card instead.
- **Title**: the name of the film.
- **Year and runtime**: when it was released and how long it runs, in minutes.
- **Tags**: the format of our copy and the language of the film.

---

## Opening a title

Click any card to see everything we know about it:

- **Lead actor** and **lead actress**: the stars of the film.
- **Director** and **music director**: the people behind the camera and the score.
- **Media type**: what kind of title it is.
- **Disk name**: the name our copy goes by on the shelf.
- **Format**: the type of disc or media we own.
- **Disk features**: the extras that come with our copy.
- **Watch status**: whether we've watched it yet.
- **Decade**: the era it belongs to.
- **Genre**: the tags that describe its style.
- **View on TMDB**: a button that opens the film's page on [The Movie Database](https://www.themoviedb.org/) in a new tab, where you can find the plot, cast, trailers and reviews.
